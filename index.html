Welcome to Morphix. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what choices you may have when you use Morphix.

Depending on how you use Morphix, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, information collected automatically, and information provided by third-party services.
